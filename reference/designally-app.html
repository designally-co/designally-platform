Kick-off framing: which tone for which audience? Investors and communities may need different registers.

For the creative team

  1. The real tension is scale versus roots — international credibility and southern-Thai identity. The winning direction bridges both rather than picking one.
  2. Every audience named is B2B: industrial buyers, logistics clients, port and warehouse users. Speak to procurement decision-makers, never consumers.
  3. The CEO is the confirmed decision maker — weight his answers, but surface the positioning conflict openly so all voices are heard before he decides.
  4. No competitor offers the same total solution. The integrated model itself is the thing to visualise.

How to run the room

🔒 INTERNAL — never shown to the client

Positioning: prepare two quick moodboards. Let Khun Krit and Khun Siriporn speak first; invite the CEO to decide last, so other voices are heard before the final call.

Tone: frame it as a spectrum, not a choice. Ask each person to place a dot. If the room splits, propose formal for investors and warmer for community content.
