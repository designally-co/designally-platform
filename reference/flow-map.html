Only two decisions belong to your team

Everything else is either the client's work or the app's. These two are deliberately human, and neither can be automated away.

DECISION 1

Is this enough to work with?

Only a person knows whether four answers from the right people beat ten from the wrong ones. The app watches the silence and asks, but it never decides.

DECISION 2

Is this brief true?

The AI can mistake two wordings of the same idea for a disagreement — especially across Thai and English. Fifteen minutes of a human reading it is what makes the output trustworthy.

What happens without anyone touching it

The reason the daily screen is usually empty.

  • The right questionnaire is attached when the package is chosen
  • Answers are collected and counted, however many arrive
  • A project is raised to Needs you after 5 quiet days
  • The brief is written — agreements, conflicts, gaps, deck outline
  • Conflicts become DECIDE slides, placed early in the deck
  • Content volume is calculated from pages × languages
  • Finished projects move to the archive 14 days after the kick-off

Where you see all of this

Two screens and one drawer. That is the entire app.

  • Today — only what is waiting on your team. Usually two or three lines, sometimes empty.
  • Projects — every live client, their stage, who has answered, how long since. Click one to open it.
  • Library — the question templates and finished projects. Opened a few times a year.
