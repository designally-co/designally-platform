# Website questionnaire — revised draft (v2)

**Designally · August 2026**

Built from your current Website Survey. Bugs fixed, stakeholder identity added, and a content-and-ownership section that doesn't exist today. Thai wording for the shared questions is taken verbatim from your existing branding form so nothing drifts between the two.

**Structure:** five blocks. A branding-only project uses blocks A and B. A website project uses A, B, C, D and — only if they sell online — E. A combined project uses everything and answers block B **once**.

| Block | Questions | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| A · About you | 3 | everyone |
| B · Core brand | 10 | everyone |
| C · Your website | 15 | website projects |
| D · Content and ownership | 9 | website projects |
| E · Selling online | 8 | only if e-commerce |

Roughly 25 minutes for a standard website project, 30 with e-commerce.
Changes are marked ⊕ new · ✎ changed · ✕ removed.

## Block A · About you
*Short intro: "Anyone on your team can answer this. Please pass the link to whoever should have a say." · ส่งลิงก์นี้ต่อให้ผู้ที่ควรมีส่วนร่วมได้เลย*

**A1 ⊕ Your name**
ชื่อของคุณ
*Short text · required*

**A2 ⊕ Your role or position**
ตำแหน่งของคุณ
*Short text · required*

**A3 ⊕ Are you a final decision maker for this project?**
คุณเป็นผู้ตัดสินใจขั้นสุดท้ายของโปรเจกต์นี้หรือไม่?
*Multiple choice · required*
- Yes — final decisions come to me · ใช่ ฉันเป็นผู้ตัดสินใจขั้นสุดท้าย
- Shared — we decide as a group · ตัดสินใจร่วมกับผู้อื่น
- No — I contribute my perspective · ไม่ ฉันให้มุมมองประกอบ

> **Why this matters.** Without it, when two stakeholders disagree the analysis cannot tell you whose answer carries more weight. Every conflict stays unresolvable. This is three questions and thirty seconds, and it makes the whole analysis work.

## Block D ⊕ Content and ownership
**This entire block is new.**

*Intro shown to the client: "These questions are the difference between a website that launches on time and one that waits. Please answer them even where the answer is 'not decided yet' — that is useful too." · คำถามชุดนี้คือสิ่งที่ทำให้เว็บไซต์เปิดตัวได้ตรงเวลา*

> **Why this block exists.** Your Solramari content sheet holds 1,379 rows of website content. English was filled in 853 of them. **Thai was filled in 59** — four percent. The same pattern shows in Livana and Fresh Ready, with rows sitting on `Content Needed`, `ยังไม่มี content`, and `Content Needed (อีเมลปลายทางที่รับข้อมูลจากฟอร์ม)`. The current survey asks how many languages the site needs, but never asks who writes the second one. These nine questions ask it up front.

**D1 · Who will write the website text?**
ใครเป็นผู้เขียนเนื้อหาบนเว็บไซต์?
*Multiple choice · required*
- We write it ourselves · เราเขียนเอง
- We'd like Designally to write it · ต้องการให้ Designally เขียนให้
- A third party writes it · มีผู้เขียนภายนอก
- Not decided yet · ยังไม่ได้ตัดสินใจ

**D2 · How much of the text already exists?**
เนื้อหาพร้อมแล้วมากน้อยเพียงใด?
*Multiple choice · required*
- All of it is written · เขียนครบแล้ว
- Some of it · บางส่วน
- Nothing yet · ยังไม่ได้เริ่ม

**D3 · If the site has more than one language, who produces the other language, and by when?**
หากเว็บไซต์มีมากกว่า 1 ภาษา ใครเป็นผู้จัดทำภาษาที่สอง และเสร็จเมื่อไร?
*Paragraph · required if C5 has more than one language*

> This is the single highest-value question in the questionnaire. The 4% figure above is what happens when nobody has answered it.

**D4 · Who supplies photography and video?**
ใครเป็นผู้จัดหาภาพถ่ายและวิดีโอ?

- We need everything produced · ต้องผลิตใหม่ทั้งหมด

- Not decided yet · ยังไม่ได้ตัดสินใจ

*Multiple choice · required*

## What this makes possible

Because languages, page count, budget, launch date, features and SKU count are now all countable, the analysis can produce two things it couldn't before.

**A feasibility check on day one.** Twenty pages, three languages, e-commerce with 300 SKUs, launching in six weeks, on a fixed budget — the brief says so before anyone quotes.

**A content volume forecast.** Your Solramari project ran about 46 content rows per page across two languages. A client answering "15 pages, Thai and English" implies roughly **690 pieces of content to write** — and block D says who is writing them.

That number, said out loud in the kick-off meeting, is worth more than any moodboard.
