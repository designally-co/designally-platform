# Content & Ownership survey — the follow-up

**Designally · website projects · August 2026**

Block D moves out of the main questionnaire and becomes a second, shorter survey sent later in the project. This is the right call for three reasons, and it changes more than just the timing.

---

## Why it works better as a follow-up

**It needs different people.** The discovery survey goes to brand stakeholders — the founder, the marketing head, whoever has an opinion about how the brand should feel. Almost none of them know who owns the domain, which email should receive form submissions, or whether anyone has a Shopee account. Those answers live with an operations or marketing coordinator. Asking a CEO about hosting produces "not sure", which teaches us nothing.

**It needs a settled scope.** How much content there is depends on how many pages the site has, and the page count is only really agreed at the kick-off. Asking "who writes the Thai" before anyone knows whether it's twelve pages or thirty is asking someone to commit to an unknown quantity.

**It keeps the first survey short.** The discovery survey stays around twenty minutes, which is already at the limit of what a busy client will finish thoughtfully. Adding nine operational questions to the end is where completion rates go to die.

---

## When it goes out

Sent **immediately after the kick-off meeting**, while the scope is fresh and the client is engaged.

By that point the team knows the page count and language count, so the survey can open with a real number: *"Your website will need roughly 690 pieces of written content across Thai and English."* A client answering "who writes this?" with that figure in front of them answers far more carefully than one answering in the abstract.

Sent to **one or two named people**, not the whole stakeholder group. The kick-off is where the team asks who that should be.

---

## The survey

Roughly 8 minutes. Twelve questions.

**Intro shown to the client:**

> Your website needs **[X] pieces of written content** across **[languages]**, spread over **[N] pages**.
> This short survey works out who is producing each part, and by when. It is the difference between a website that launches on schedule and one that waits.
> Please answer even where the answer is "not decided yet" — knowing that is useful to us.
>
> เว็บไซต์ของคุณต้องใช้เนื้อหาประมาณ **[X]** ชิ้น ใน **[ภาษา]** จำนวน **[N]** หน้า
> แบบสอบถามสั้น ๆ นี้ช่วยระบุว่าใครเป็นผู้จัดทำแต่ละส่วน และเสร็จเมื่อไร
> หากยังไม่ได้ตัดสินใจ โปรดระบุว่ายังไม่ตัดสินใจ ข้อมูลนี้มีประโยชน์กับเราเช่นกัน

---

### Who is answering

**1 · Your name and role**
ชื่อและตำแหน่งของคุณ
*Short text · required*

**2 ⊕ Who is our single point of contact for website content?**
ใครคือผู้ประสานงานหลักด้านเนื้อหาเว็บไซต์?
*Short text · required · helper: "Name and email. One person we can ask when something is missing." · ชื่อและอีเมล ผู้ที่เราติดต่อได้เมื่อเนื้อหาขาด*

> New, and the most useful question in the survey. Every content sheet that stalls has the same cause: nobody was named. When there is one person, gaps get chased. When there are five, nobody chases.

---

### Words

**3 · Who will write the website text?**
ใครเป็นผู้เขียนเนื้อหาบนเว็บไซต์?
*Multiple choice · required*
- We write it ourselves · เราเขียนเอง
- We'd like Designally to write it · ต้องการให้ Designally เขียนให้
- A third party writes it · มีผู้เขียนภายนอก
- Not decided yet · ยังไม่ได้ตัดสินใจ

**4 · How much of the text already exists?**
เนื้อหาพร้อมแล้วมากน้อยเพียงใด?
*Multiple choice · required*
- All of it is written · เขียนครบแล้ว
- Some of it · บางส่วน
- Nothing yet · ยังไม่ได้เริ่ม

**5 · Who produces the second language, and by when?**
ใครเป็นผู้จัดทำภาษาที่สอง และเสร็จเมื่อไร?
*Paragraph · required when the site has more than one language*

> The most important question in the project. Solramari: 1,379 content rows, English filled 853, **Thai filled 59**. Nobody had answered this.

**6 ⊕ Realistically, when will the content be ready?**
ตามความเป็นจริง เนื้อหาจะพร้อมเมื่อไร?
*Multiple choice · required*
- Within 2 weeks · ภายใน 2 สัปดาห์
- 3–4 weeks · 3–4 สัปดาห์
- 1–2 months · 1–2 เดือน
- More than 2 months · มากกว่า 2 เดือน
- We don't know yet · ยังไม่ทราบ

> New. Compared against the launch date from the discovery survey, this is an immediate, honest warning — and it comes from the client's own mouth, not from the agency guessing.

---

### Pictures

**7 · Who supplies photography and video?**
ใครเป็นผู้จัดหาภาพถ่ายและวิดีโอ?
*Multiple choice · required*
- We have finished assets ready · มีไฟล์พร้อมแล้ว
- We have some, and need more produced · มีบางส่วน ต้องผลิตเพิ่ม
- We need everything produced · ต้องผลิตใหม่ทั้งหมด
- We'll use stock images · ใช้ภาพสำเร็จรูป
- Not decided yet · ยังไม่ได้ตัดสินใจ

### Documents

**8 · Who writes the legal pages — privacy, cookies, terms, delivery, returns?**
ใครเป็นผู้จัดทำหน้าเอกสารทางกฎหมาย เช่น นโยบายความเป็นส่วนตัว คุกกี้ เงื่อนไขการใช้งาน การจัดส่ง การคืนสินค้า?
*Multiple choice · required*
- We already have them · มีอยู่แล้ว
- Our legal team will write them · ทีมกฎหมายของเราจัดทำ
- We'd like Designally to draft them · ต้องการให้ Designally ร่างให้
- Not decided yet · ยังไม่ได้ตัดสินใจ

### Plumbing

**9 · Which email address should receive messages from the website forms?**
อีเมลใดที่จะรับข้อมูลจากฟอร์มบนเว็บไซต์?
*Short text · required*

**10 · Which of these accounts already exist?**
บัญชีใดที่มีอยู่แล้วบ้าง?
*Checkboxes*
- LINE Official Account · Google Business Profile (Maps) · Facebook · Instagram · TikTok · Lazada · Shopee · Payment gateway · Google Analytics · None yet ยังไม่มี

**11 · Who owns the domain name and hosting at the moment?**
ปัจจุบันใครเป็นเจ้าของโดเมนและโฮสติ้ง?
*Short text · required · helper: "If you're not sure, say so — we'll help you find out." · หากไม่แน่ใจ ระบุได้ เราช่วยตรวจสอบให้*

**12 · Who will look after the website after launch, and will they need to edit content themselves?**
ใครดูแลเว็บไซต์หลังเปิดตัว และต้องแก้ไขเนื้อหาเองหรือไม่?
*Multiple choice · required*
- Our team will edit content ourselves · ทีมเราแก้ไขเอง
- We'd like Designally to maintain it · ต้องการให้ Designally ดูแล
- It rarely changes, no editing needed · ไม่ค่อยเปลี่ยน ไม่ต้องแก้ไข
- Not decided yet · ยังไม่ได้ตัดสินใจ

## What comes out of it

This survey does not feed the kick-off brief — that meeting has already happened. It produces a different, shorter document: a **Content Readiness Plan**.

**A verdict, first line.** On track, at risk, or blocked — from comparing the content-ready date against the launch date.

**The owner map.** Every category of content with a named owner and a date: copy, second language, photography, legal pages, product data. Anything without an owner is listed as a gap, because unowned work is the work that doesn't happen.

**The unresolved list.** Everything answered "not decided yet", gathered in one place, addressed to the named content coordinator. This is the follow-up email, written for the team.

**The plumbing checklist.** Form inbox, domain, hosting, existing accounts and who holds the credentials — the small items that hold up a launch on the final day.

---

## What this makes possible later

The platform already knows the page count, the language count and the feature list. Once it also knows the owners, it has everything needed to **generate the Content Sheet automatically** — the tabs, the page rows, the `Section Name` and `Content Section` columns, the EN and TH columns, pre-marked with `Content Needed` and the owner's name.

Your team builds that sheet by hand today, once per project. It is the same structure every time. Worth putting on the Phase 2 list.

---

## What this changes in the platform

Website projects gain two stages the branding flow doesn't have:

**Branding:** Lead → Proposal → Survey → Analysis → Kick-off
**Website and combined:** Lead → Proposal → Survey → Analysis → Kick-off → **Content** → **Build**

So the app carries two survey types. The discovery survey goes to many stakeholders when the deal is signed. The content survey goes to one or two named people right after the kick-off. Both live under the same project, and the worklist raises the second one as an action the moment a kick-off is marked done.
