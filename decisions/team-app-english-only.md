## Decision

Every label, button, message and validation the team app draws for itself is English. Data is shown exactly as it arrives, often in Thai. The client survey stays bilingual from the identity card onwards.

## Why

The bilingual pairs doubled the length of every confirmation for readers who had already read the first half.
